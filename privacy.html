Who we are

Day One Parental Leave Australia is a campaign led by Dr Elizabeth Watt.

What we collect

When you sign, we collect your first and last name, email address, and (if you choose) your mobile number and your story. We also record whether you want email updates and whether you want your name kept off the website.

How we use it

We use your details to count signatures, to contact you about the campaign if you ticked the email updates box, and, with your consent, to share your story or name. We will not publish your name if you tick “Don’t publish my name on the website”.

Who sees it

Your details are processed by the form service we use to receive sign-ups (Web3Forms) and by our email tool, and are kept in the campaign’s accounts. We do not sell your information.

Unsubscribe, access and removal

Every email will have an unsubscribe link. To see, correct or delete your details, email hello@dayoneparentalleave.au.
