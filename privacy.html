Privacy policy

Last updated 25 November 2026.

Who we are

Day One Parental Leave Australia is a campaign led by Dr Elizabeth Watt. In this policy, “we” means the campaign. You can reach us at hello@dayoneparentalleave.au.

What we collect

If you sign the petition, we collect your first and last name and your email address. If you choose to, you can also give us your mobile number and your story. We also record whether you asked for email updates, text updates, or for your name to be kept off the website.

If you only visit the site, we do not ask for or collect any personal information.

How we use it

We use your details to count signatures, to show decision-makers how many people support the campaign, and to contact you about the campaign if you asked for updates. We use your mobile number only if you ticked the text updates box.

Names and stories

We may publish signatories’ names on this website. If you tick “Don’t publish my name on the website”, we will count your signature but not show your name. We will not publish your story without checking with you first, and you can ask us to publish it without your name.

Who handles your information

Sign-ups are received through a form service (Web3Forms) and arrive in the campaign’s email inbox, which is run through our email providers. These providers may store information on servers outside Australia. We do not sell your information, and we do not share it with anyone else, except where the law requires us to.

Cookies and other services

This website does not use advertising or tracking cookies. It loads fonts from Google Fonts, which means Google receives your IP address when the page loads.

How long we keep it

We keep sign-up details while the campaign is running, and for a reasonable time afterwards. If you ask us to delete your details, we will.

Your choices

You can ask us at any time to see, correct or delete the information we hold about you, to remove your name or story from the website, or to stop sending you updates. Every email will have an unsubscribe link. Email hello@dayoneparentalleave.au and we will respond within 30 days.

Concerns

We aim to handle your information in line with the Australian Privacy Principles. If you are unhappy with how we have done so, please contact us first. You can also complain to the Office of the Australian Information Commissioner at oaic.gov.au.

Changes

If we change this policy, we will update the date at the top of this page.
